### 9.1 What SaaS Actually Requires (Elementally)

SaaS is a **Water + Wood** business at its core, with Metal as a substrate and only modest Earth/Fire involvement.

| Element | SaaS function | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Water (수)** | Recurring revenue flow, customer-success motion, data pipelines, distribution, audience, churn management | SaaS is fundamentally a **flow business** — the MRR/ARR rhythm, the renewal cadence, the customer-cohort churn curves. **This is the central element.** |
| **Wood (목)** | Product strategy, learning/onboarding, growth loops, content marketing, A/B experimentation, customer education | SaaS is a **learning/iteration business** — continuous product improvement, growth experiments, user education. **This is the secondary element.** |
| **Metal (금)** | Code precision, API contracts, security/compliance, billing/finops, "the rules" of the platform | The **delivery substrate** — necessary but not customer-facing. |
| **Earth (토)** | Hosting, infrastructure, real-cost substrate | Modest presence; Cloud (AWS/GCP) absorbs most of this. |
| **Fire (화)** | Brand, marketing, founder presence, demos, conference presence | Moderate; depends on segment. |

**The classical SaaS archetype** is therefore **Water-anchored + Wood-anchored** — the **opposite** of the trio's joint profile, which is **Earth + Metal + Fire**-anchored.

- **Vertical SaaS for tier-2/3 South-India is underserved** — most B2B SaaS targets tier-1 metros (Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi). The trio's geographic alignment is an unfair advantage.

> **Generic SaaS is the trio's weakest cross-candidate industry. Vertical SaaS for the trio's own F&B / Real Estate / Hospitality industries — and EdTech for hospitality training — are the only SaaS configurations that work, and only as secondary plays to the primary F&B / Real Estate venture. The trio should not start a SaaS company; if they end up building one, it should be a tool they built for themselves first.**
