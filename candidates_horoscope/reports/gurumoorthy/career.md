  - All ten-domain ratings are **classical-element-based**, not financial projections. The classical reading is a **tendency**, not a forecast.
  - The ratings are **derived from the natal chart's element fit, day-master profile, ten-god distribution, and current time-window**. The querent's **personal interest, capital availability, family situation, and health** are not factored in — the querent must overlay those.
  - The 5-star scale is **ordinal, not cardinal** — a 4★ vs 4.5★ distinction is **fine-grained, not a precise ranking**.
  - "Business-fit" reading assumes the querent is **making the decisions**; if the querent is **advising a son, daughter, or partner**, the rating should be **re-derived for that person's chart**.
