## Business Domain Fit

Per `knowledge/13-wealth-and-business.md`'s 식상생재 (食傷生財, "Output feeds Wealth") chain check:

- **식상 is present and real** (壬 상관 in the year stem, 癸 식신 hidden in the hour) — a genuine output channel: writing, analysis, teaching, or built-product work.
- **재성 is present but thin** (乙 편재 visible in month, 甲 정재 hidden in day) — this is enough for the chain to run (the base report's own "Income Rhythm" already reads this correctly as "steady increments... rarely interrupted by surprise windfalls"), but not enough to support a high-capital, deal-volume-driven business model.
- **The chain runs**: output-based income is structurally supported. Per `knowledge/13`, this is read as "highly favorable for entrepreneurial types," conditional on the Day Master being strong enough to sustain the output — Harish's Balanced (not weak) reading supports this.
- **재생관 (Wealth funds status) is only lightly formed**: with 관성 thin (1, hidden), turning earnings into institutional rank is a secondary story, not the chart's main engine.

**What kind of business, specifically:**

- **Best fit: a solo or small-team advisory/consulting practice** — a credentialed independent practice (strategy, policy, compliance, or analytical-research consulting) built around the querent's own expertise, monetized through delivered analysis or advisory retainers rather than a scaled product business.
- **Product vs. service:** service-and-expertise-led, not product-manufacturing-led — the chart's Metal is native precision/judgement, not Earth-native asset-building or Fire-native brand-building.
- **Institutional-backed vs. fully independent:** a *hybrid* path fits best — build credibility inside an institution or established firm first (the single 관성 gives a real, if modest, institutional-authority thread to draw on), then move to independent practice once the 식상생재 channel (a body of published work, a track record of delivered analysis) is established. This matches `knowledge/12`'s Employment vs. Entrepreneurship signal for a chart whose 관성 is thin but present: institutional credibility first, independence second — not simultaneously.
- **Partnership structure:** 비겁 is present (3, all hidden — no visible 겁재) but not dominant. Per `knowledge/13`'s 겁재奪財 pattern, this is a **mild, not acute**, partnership-risk signal. A **sole practice or one clearly-defined co-founder with a written operating agreement** is safer than a loosely-structured multi-partner firm, but the chart does not show the pronounced peer-competition risk some charts carry — partnership itself is not contraindicated, only *undefined* partnership.

## Employment vs. Entrepreneurship — Synthesis

Combining the ten-god weight (관성 thinnest, 인성/식상 strongest) with the timing picture above: this chart is not built for either pure lifelong employment or an immediate high-burn venture. The best-supported arc is **sequential, not simultaneous** — institutional or firm-based employment through the 30s and 40s (using the thin-but-real 관성 and the currently-neutral decades for credibility-building), transitioning to independent or advisory practice once the 50s Water decade opens and the 식상생재 track record is established. Attempting full independence in the current (30–39) decade is not chart-contraindicated, but the chart offers less structural tailwind for it than it will a decade later.
