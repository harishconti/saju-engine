## Business Domain Fit

- **Best fit: a solo or small-team advisory/consulting practice** — a credentialed independent practice (strategy, policy, compliance, or analytical-research consulting) built around the querent's own expertise, monetized through delivered analysis or advisory retainers rather than a scaled product business.
- **Product vs. service:** service-and-expertise-led, not product-manufacturing-led — the chart's Metal is native precision and judgment, not Earth-native asset-building or Fire-native brand-building.
- **Institutional-backed vs. fully independent:** a *hybrid* path fits best — build credibility inside an institution or established firm first (the single 관성 gives a real, if modest, institutional-authority thread to draw on), then move to independent practice once the 식상생재 channel (a body of published work, a track record of delivered analysis) is established. - **Partnership structure:** 비겁 is present (3, all hidden except the day-pillar self, no visible 겁재) but not dominant. Per `knowledge/13`'s 겁재奪財 pattern, this is a **mild, not acute**, partnership-risk signal. A **sole practice or one clearly-defined co-founder with a written operating agreement** is safer than a loosely-structured multi-partner firm, but the chart does not show the pronounced peer-competition risk some charts carry — partnership itself is not contraindicated, only *undefined* partnership.

## Employment vs. Entrepreneurship — Synthesis

Combining the ten-god weight (관성 thinnest, 인성/식상 strongest) with the timing picture above: this chart is not built for either pure lifelong employment or an immediate high-burn venture. The best-supported arc is **sequential, not simultaneous** — institutional or firm-based employment through the 30s and 40s (using the thin-but-real 관성 and this era's Metal-희신 support for credibility-building), transitioning to independent or advisory practice once the 50s Water decade opens and the 식상생재 track record is established. Full independence earlier than that is not chart-contraindicated, but the chart's structural tailwind for it strengthens materially a decade or two out.
