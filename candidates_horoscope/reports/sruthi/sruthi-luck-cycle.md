This report walks your whole life in **five-year blocks**. Each 10-year major-luck period (대운) is split into two five-year phases, and each phase is read from the five concrete annual pillars (세운) that fall inside it, together with the decade's own stem and branch. Sections cover wealth, business, family and investment patterns in each block. Read the blocks as *rhythms and tendencies*, not fixed events *(see knowledge/08-luck-pillars.md Part 5)*.
