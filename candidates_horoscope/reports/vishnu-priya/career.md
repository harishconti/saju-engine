## 1. Core Career DNA

Your chart is built around a **Sin Metal (辛)** Day Master with a balanced strength reading. Yin Metal is the image of the polished jewel or fine instrument: it succeeds through precision, refinement, and quiet competence. *(see knowledge/01-stems.md)*.

The strongest career path is one where:

1. You can **refine something** — data, language, craft, diagnosis, design, code, or systems.
2. The work has **clear standards of quality** — vague or purely political environments will frustrate your Metal nature.
3. There is **enough structure** to protect your focus — the Bing Direct Officer in your hour pillar respects rules and explicit expectations.
4. There is **a Wood or Water payoff** — your favorable Water and supporting Wood give your Metal useful targets and outlets.

## 4. Employment vs. Entrepreneurship

- **Employment (early to mid-career):** Favorable while you are in Fire/Metal 대운 periods (20–39). Look for organizations with clear career ladders, merit-based promotion, and specialized roles where your precision is valued. Avoid flat or chaotic startups where you would be asked to perform many ill-defined functions.
- **Entrepreneurship:** Best supported from the Mu-Sul / Gi-Hae periods onward, when Earth/Water luck provides structure and a creative outlet. If you start earlier, partner with someone strong in Water/Wood (strategy, client development, planning) to compensate for your natal scarcity of those elements.

## 5. Practical Recommendations

1. Choose a Tier-1 domain early and build depth; your wealth path depends on recognized skill.
2. Prioritize roles with clear quality metrics and feedback loops.
3. Guard against Fire-induced burnout — the Bing-Shen period and 2026 Bing-Wu year bring authority pressure.
4. Avoid overcompetition with peers — the Shen branch brings Robber energy; rivalry is possible, but your strength is refinement, not dominance contests.
5. Leverage 2025 (Yi-Si) and 2028 (Mu-Shen) — both support Water and consolidate your current major luck branch.
