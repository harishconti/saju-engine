# Cosmic Saju — Market Research (September 2026)

> **Purpose:** an auditable fact base for the go-to-market decisions in `../improvements_issues.md`.
> **Method:** 15 web searches / direct fetches on 2026-09-07, cross-checked against the deep-dive
> research report at `research-engine/output/2026-09-07/report-saju.html`. Every claim below carries
> a confidence flag and a source.
> **Confidence:** `[H]` primary source or corroborated across ≥2 independent sources · `[M]` single
> credible source · `[L]` single self-interested source, or directional only.
>
> **Bottom line:** the demand is real but shallow, the price floor is already automated by a funded
> competitor, and manual fulfilment caps this at a **~$1–3K/month side business**. It is worth
> validating; it is not a startup. Every optimistic number in the source report survives scrutiny
> only with caveats attached — those caveats are the point of this document.

## 1. Demand & audience

### 1.1 US engagement is broad but shallow — `[H]`

- **30% of US adults** consult astrology, tarot, or a fortune-teller at least once a year (Pew
  Research Center, fielded 21–27 Oct 2024, released 21 May 2025, n = 9,593).
- Breakdown: **astrology 28%**, tarot 11%, fortune-tellers 6%.
- **Only 10%** engage "because they believe the practices give helpful insights." **20% do it "just
  for fun."**
- **1% rely on it "a lot"** when making important decisions; a further 5% "a little."
- 46% of women 18–49 engage (the report's persona anchor).

**Interpretation:** this is a large *entertainment* audience, not a large *belief* audience. Implication
for the business: high impulse-purchase potential at low price points, **low repeat rate, low LTV**,
and a customer who will not tolerate a report that reads as machine-generated at a craft price.

> Query: `Pew Research 2025 Americans astrology tarot fortune telling percentage`
> Source: https://www.pewresearch.org/religion/2025/05/21/3-in-10-americans-consult-astrology-tarot-cards-or-fortune-tellers/

### 1.2 The K-culture tailwind is real — `[H]` — but it is fandom, not proven purchase intent

- **225 million Hallyu fans worldwide** as of Dec 2023 (Korea Foundation + Ministry of Foreign
  Affairs, report 12 Mar 2024, 119 countries, 1,748 fan clubs). Up 25.8% YoY.
- **The Americas is the fastest-growing region** — Mexico alone +~80% YoY. Asia/Oceania still hold
  66% of fans.
- ~68% of fan clubs are K-pop-focused, ~10% K-drama.

> Query: `Korea Foundation 225 million Hallyu fans worldwide 2023`
> Sources: https://www.korea.net/NewsFocus/Society/view?articleId=248349 ·
> https://kf.or.kr/kfNewsletter/mgzinSubViewPage.do?mgzinSubSn=27283&langTy=ENG

### 1.3 The K-occult media wave — `[H]`

- **KPop Demon Hunters** (Netflix, released 20 Jun 2025): ~210M views by late 2025, later reported
  266M+ — on track to be Netflix's most-watched film ever. The plot centres on a *mudang* (shaman)
  lineage and the *gut* ritual.
- **Battle of Fates** (Disney+): 49 seers compete for a ₩100M (~$69,500) prize; the biggest Disney+
  Korea premiere. Fan-meet tickets for four of its shamans sold out in **two minutes** in Thailand.
- Korean media (Korea JoongAng Daily, Korea Times, Korea Bizwire) describe a broad "shamanism
  entertainment boom" with foreign clients at saju cafés and K-shaman events selling out overseas.

**Caveat `[M]`:** every source describes *cultural interest* and *content consumption*. None
demonstrates that this converts to **paid saju-report purchases** by Western consumers. The wave is a
reason the timing is favourable, not evidence of a specific revenue opportunity.

### 2.1 Korean fortune-telling market: ~$1B, and the bigger number is stale — `[M]`

- **₩1.4 trillion (~US$988M) as of 2024**, per startup-analysis firm InnoForest (cited via
  marketsize.net and Seoulz).

**Use ~$1B, flagged as possibly undercounting the informal cash economy.** Do not cite $3.7B.

**Cheok Cheok / sajuplus.com** (operated by Samwonsoft, a Korean software company), fetched directly
2026-09-07:

| Product | Price |
|---|---|
| Personalized Saju Reading | **$9.99** |
| Saju Compatibility Reading | **$8.99** |
| 2026 Year Ahead Reading | **$7.99** |
| Tarot Master Report | **$2.99** |

- **7 free tools**: birth-chart calculator, today's fortune, birth-date reading, tarot, yearly
  fortune, dream interpretation, five-elements calendar.
- Delivery: **instant, by email**, PayPal checkout. AI-generated.
- This is the closest competitor to the intended impulse tier, and it is fully automated. **You

### 3.2 saju.com English launch — real, traction unproven — `[M]`

- **saju.com** (Korean platform, 5M Korean users, reportedly #1 on the ChatGPT Store) launched an

### 3.3 The three price bands — `[M]`

| Band | Price | Who | Delivery |
|---|---|---|---|
| Impulse | $8–30 | Cheok Cheok, K-Saju ($10.99), Sajumuse ($29, was $39) | instant – 24h, AI/semi-auto |
| Mid (human-assisted) | $30–70 | Saju Atelier ($39–69, named master), Fiverr Korean masters ($15–50), Etsy ($19–70) | 1–5 days |
| Premium (hand-written) | $75–150 | Bazi Candle, Tinhan ($120), Lumen Tao, CrystalStones | 3 min – 5 days |

- Sajumuse: two reports $78 separately, **$55 bundled** (couple + personal).
- Fiverr Korean-saju sellers explicitly cite *KPop Demon Hunters* as a demand driver; tiers run
  10-page/1-question basic → 60+ page/3-question premium.
- Seoul in-person sessions: **$30–300**, ~1 hour; ₩5,000–30,000 (~$4–23) for budget options.

**The gap you can occupy:** hand-crafted depth at the **mid band ($19–69)**, because the impulse tier
is being commoditised by AI and the premium tier has waitlists. The **compatibility product** is the
sharpest differentiator — no English-language competitor publishes a composite score + 11-sub-system
structure + per-partner snapshots + couple timing overlay.

## 4. Acquisition channel

### 4.1 Organic short-form video is the proven CAC≈0 path — `[M]` for the pattern, `[L]` for the numbers

- **Starcrossed** reached a reported **$70K MRR in ~90 days** via founder TikTok.
  **Caveats, all load-bearing:**
  1. The founder (@nedatheastrologer) spent **~2 years and 163M views** building an audience
     *before* the 90-day window. It was **not a cold start.**
  2. Starcrossed is an **AI "soulmate-drawing" subscription app**, not a report business. Its unit
     economics (recurring subscription, near-zero marginal cost) do not transfer to manual PDF
     fulfilment.
  3. The $70K figure is **founder-reported**, repeated across secondary growth-marketing blogs
     (Medium, Social Growth Engineers, Venture Radar). No audited source.
- **The transferable finding** is the *pattern*, and it is well corroborated: in this niche, organic
  short-form video is the lowest-CAC acquisition channel, and it works **content-first, not
  ad-first** (Forbes on "Moonlight & Sage": $5 readings → six-figure shop via "witchy TikTok"; Seoulz
  notes low CAC / organic word-of-mouth as a structural feature of the Korean category).

> Query: `Starcrossed astrology app $70K MRR TikTok 90 days founder`
> Sources: https://www.socialgrowthengineers.com/this-random-girl-built-a-70k-mrr-astrology-app-in-90-days ·
> https://medium.com/@thesaucedepot/this-random-girl-built-an-astrology-app-to-70-000-mrr-in-90-days-cafb41661bbe

### 4.2 Conversion rates — use cross-industry numbers, discounted — `[H]`

- **Median dedicated landing page: 4.02%** (LanderLab, 2026 data). WordStream: median **2.35%**, top
  25% ≥ 5.31%, top 10% ≥ 11.45%.
- **No astrology/spirituality-specific benchmark exists** in public data.
- Three-field forms convert ~25% better than nine-field forms (relevant: the intake form).

**Planning assumption: 1–2% on cold traffic from social**, 3%+ only after the offer and page are
tuned. The deep-dive report's "2–4%" is the optimistic end of a cross-industry range, not a
niche-proven figure.

> Query: `digital product landing page conversion rate benchmark 2026 spirituality astrology`
> Source: https://landerlab.io/blog/landing-page-conversion-rate

### 4.3 Etsy — a validation channel, not a business — `[M]`

- Buyers do search Etsy for "BaZi reading" / "saju reading". A single IG-viral seller can reach
  $1K+/mo.
- But the **median metaphysical shop earns < $100/month**, and income scales with hours worked, not
  sales. Etsy also periodically deactivates metaphysical listings.
- Use Etsy purely as a **zero-CAC demand test**, always driving buyers to an owned email list.

> Query: `Etsy metaphysical spiritual reading shop average income 2025`
> Source: https://www.starterstory.com/ (metaphysical store profitability) + Etsy marketplace pages

### 5.2 MCP monetisation is early and mostly unsuccessful — `[H]`

- **12,000+ public MCP servers as of March 2026; fewer than 5% monetise.** "Most make $0."
- Realistic first-year target for a well-positioned server: **$500–5,000/month**. 21st.dev hit $10K
  MRR in 6 weeks (an outlier).
- Winning pattern: **free MCP server in front of a paid API** (Bright Data / Exa / Tavily model).
- Marketplaces: MCP Marketplace (Stripe Connect), MCPize (80% rev share, hosting + billing), Apify
  (80% minus compute, pay-per-event).

**Verdict:** revisit only when B2C has proven the narrative asset has weekly active users. Trigger
condition in `../improvements_issues.md` §10.

> Sources: https://mcp-marketplace.io/blog/state-of-mcp-monetization-2026 ·

## 6. Why India is "later / skip" — `[H]`

- **AstroTalk** (the dominant Indian platform): FY25 revenue **₹1,214 crore (~US$145M)**, **+85%
  YoY** (from ₹656 Cr in FY24); ARR ~₹1,600 Cr as of Aug 2025.
- **~80% share of India's online astrology market**; 60M+ users; 41,000+ astrologers; 1.5M
  transacting users; 25–30% repeat rate.
- **IPO expected late 2026 / early 2027** — India's first listed consumer astrology-tech platform.
- Its e-commerce arm alone did ₹140 Cr in 2025.

The Indian market is (a) **huge and growing**, (b) **~80% controlled by one soon-to-be-public
company**, (c) built on a **live talk-to-an-astrologer marketplace** model, not static PDF reports,
and (d) **Vedic-native** — Saju is a foreign system there. A solo founder selling Korean-Saju PDFs
has no wedge. India is at best a Phase-2 PPP-priced experiment surfaced through regional-language
content; it gets **zero content effort in Phase 1**.

## 7. Operations & economics

- **Craft time:** 30–90 min per report (the deep-dive report's audit of the actual generated
  readings). AI-draft + human-polish workflow targets ≤ 45 min.
- **Capacity ceiling:** ~15–25 reports/week at 15–20 fulfilment hours/week → **~$1.3–2.6K/month
  revenue ceiling** at a ~$20 blended AOV; higher with a compat/deep mix. `[M]` (arithmetic on the
  above).
- **Fixed cost of the whole operation: < $50/month** — domain (~$12/yr), Cloudflare Pages / free
  hosting, email free tier (Resend 3K/mo), marginal LLM drafting cost. The `<$500` test budget
  covers ~10 months plus small Phase-2 ad tests. `[M]`
- **Checkout:** Lemon Squeezy (acquired by Stripe in 2024) and Paddle are true Merchants of Record
  (both ~5% + $0.50), **operate from India**, handle US sales tax / EU VAT / 30+ jurisdictions
  automatically, and support region-priced variants without geo-IP plumbing. `[H]`
