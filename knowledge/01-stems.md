# 01 · Heavenly Stems (천간, 天干)

The ten Heavenly Stems are the upper characters of the four pillars. They represent the *visible* aspect of cosmic energy — the active, expressive, announcing layer of a Saju chart. The Day Master (일간, 日干) is the **single most important stem** in a chart: it represents the querent themselves.

## Stem-to-Stem Combinations (천간합)

Five pairs of stems combine (합) to produce a new element:

| Pair | Stems | Combined Element |
|---|---|---|
| 甲己合 | 갑 + 기 | Earth (土) |
| 乙庚合 | 을 + 경 | Metal (金) |
| 丙辛合 | 병 + 신 | Water (水) |
| 丁壬合 | 정 + 임 | Wood (木) |
| 戊癸合 | 무 + 계 | Fire (火) |

Conditions for a successful combination (합화):
1. Both stems must be present in the chart.
2. The combined element must be in **season** (i.e., supported by the month branch).
3. The weaker stem of the pair should be **drained** or absent from the chart.
4. The combination must not be **broken** by a clash with a stem that disrupts the pair (e.g., 甲己合 can be broken by 乙 or 庚 if 乙/庚 are powerful).

If a combination does not "transform" (합화), it is read as a **half-combination (합)** — a binding, attraction, or mutual modification without full transformation.

## Stem Clashes (천간충)

A common interpretive note: stems technically do not "clash" the way branches do. However:
- A stem that **controls** another (e.g. 金 controlling 木) creates a **제 (制, controlling)** relationship, often read as pressure or limitation.
- A stem that is **controlled by** another (e.g. 木 being controlled by 金) creates a **극 (剋, being overcome)** relationship, often read as hardship or external pressure on the querent's expression.
- A stem that **generates** another (e.g. 木 generating 火) creates a **생 (生, generating)** relationship, often read as support, nurturing, or expense (depending on the ten-god class).

## Stem-by-Stem Profile (일간 관점)

When a stem appears **in the chart**, its meaning is interpreted in relation to the Day Master as one of the Ten Gods (십신). See `05-ten-gods.md`. Below is the **inherent** character of each stem independent of the Day Master — useful when discussing the **year stem** (representing ancestors / outer persona) or the querent's deep self:

### 甲 갑 — Yang Wood (양목)
- **Image:** A tall pine, a great oak — sturdy, upright, principled.
- **Strengths:** Leadership, integrity, growth-oriented, generous, willing to start.
- **Weaknesses:** Rigid, can be self-righteous, vulnerable to being "cut down" by Metal.
- **Best condition:** Rooted in moist soil (Earth with Water), free to grow upward.

### 乙 을 — Yin Wood (음목)
- **Image:** A vine, a climbing plant, grass bending in the wind.
- **Strengths:** Flexible, adaptive, persuasive, gentle, knows how to attach to strong hosts.
- **Weaknesses:** Dependent, can be indecisive, vulnerability to being "pruned" or cut.
- **Best condition:** Anchored by solid Earth (토), lit by Fire (화) for warmth.

### 丙 병 — Yang Fire (양화)
- **Image:** The sun at noon.
- **Strengths:** Radiant, generous, magnanimous, illuminating, energetic.
- **Weaknesses:** Can scorch, can be too exposing, vulnerable to Water's "extinguishing" pressure.
- **Best condition:** Rooted in Earth (토, the sun "baking the land"), accompanied by Metal (form / structure).

### 丁 정 — Yin Fire (음화)
- **Image:** A candle, a starlight, a coal fire.
- **Strengths:** Warm, focused, persistent, illuminating small spaces with great care.
- **Weaknesses:** Vulnerable to wind (Wood) and water, can be exhausting to maintain.
- **Best condition:** Near Wood (which feeds it) but shielded from harsh wind.

### 戊 무 — Yang Earth (양토)
- **Image:** A mountain, a great plateau, a boulder.
- **Strengths:** Stable, trustworthy, capable of bearing weight, calm, generous.
- **Weaknesses:** Slow, can be immovable, vulnerable to Wood's "penetrating" force.
- **Best condition:** In a warm season (Fire supports it), in places where it is the foundation.

### 己 기 — Yin Earth (음토)
- **Image:** A garden soil, a cultivated field, a wet earth.
- **Strengths:** Nurturing, fertile, containing, supporting, accommodating.
- **Weaknesses:** Can be over-saturated (too much Water), can be muddy without Fire to dry it.
- **Best condition:** In a warm season, with Wood (to break up the soil) and Fire (to dry it).

### 庚 경 — Yang Metal (양금)
- **Image:** A sword, an axe, raw iron ore, a heavy hammer.
- **Strengths:** Decisive, sharp, justice-oriented, willing to cut through obstacles.
- **Weaknesses:** Harsh, can be cold, can hurt what it touches.
- **Best condition:** Tempered in fire (Fire refines Metal), grounded in Earth (Earth produces Metal).

### 辛 신 — Yin Metal (음금)
- **Image:** A fine jewel, a needle, a piece of cutlery.
- **Strengths:** Refined, perceptive, beautiful, exacting, capable of high precision.
- **Weaknesses:** Brittle, can be overly critical, vulnerable to Fire (which melts it).
- **Best condition:** In a cool, dry environment; accompanied by Earth to support it.

### 壬 임 — Yang Water (양수)
- **Image:** A great river, the ocean, a waterfall.
- **Strengths:** Vast, intelligent, flowing, adaptive, capable of carrying many things.
- **Weaknesses:** Unbounded, can be cold, can drown, can erode without intention.
- **Best condition:** Dammed or directed by Earth (Earth controls Water).

### 癸 계 — Yin Water (음수)
- **Image:** Rain, dew, a spring, a mist.
- **Strengths:** Quiet, persistent, nourishing, gentle, capable of great patience.
- **Weaknesses:** Can stagnate, can be dispersed, vulnerable to freezing (Earth blocks it).
- **Best condition:** In a warm climate (Fire evaporates it into clouds, then it rains); flowing.
