# 02 · Earthly Branches (지지, 地支)

The twelve Earthly Branches are the lower characters of the four pillars. They are the *hidden* aspect of cosmic energy — the substance, the timing, the relational layer. A branch is **richer** than a stem: each branch contains one to three hidden stems (지장간, 地藏干), which act as the "qi" (energy) stored inside.

## Hidden Stems (지장간, 地藏干)

Each branch contains **one to three** hidden stems, classified by their prominence:

- **본기 (本氣)** — Main Qi: the principal element of the branch. Always one. This is the loudest voice inside the branch.
- **중기 (中氣)** — Middle Qi: secondary element. Only in branches that have 3 hidden stems.
- **여기 (餘氣)** — Residual Qi: tertiary element. Only in branches that have 3 hidden stems.

|---|---|---|---|
| 子 (자) | 癸 (계, Yin Water) | — | — |
| 丑 (축) | 己 (기, Yin Earth) | 癸 (계, Yin Water) | 辛 (신, Yin Metal) |
| 寅 (인) | 甲 (갑, Yang Wood) | 丙 (병, Yang Fire) | 戊 (무, Yang Earth) |

> The hidden stems are **interpreted as ten gods** of the Day Master, just like the visible stems. The 본기 carries the most weight in the branch. When the 본기 is a powerful ally of the Day Master (e.g., 甲 day master with 寅 month), the Day Master is significantly strengthened.

## Branch Six Combinations (육합, 六合)

Six pairs of branches combine. The two branches act as a **single unit** for many readings, including: marriage, partnerships, hidden agreements, locked energy.

| Pair | Combined Element | Typical Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 子丑 (자축) | Earth (토) | Combined support, married couple ground |
| 寅亥 (인해) | Wood (목) | Combined growth, long-term venture |
| 卯戌 (묘술) | Fire (화) | Combined warmth, hidden love, alchemy |
| 辰酉 (진유) | Metal (금) | Combined precision, contractual clarity |
| 巳申 (사신) | Water (수) | Combined flow, "tortoise-fire" relationship, partnership in tech/career |
| 午未 (오미) | Earth (화/토) | Combined heat/light, sun at peak, marriage of fire & earth |

> For 육합 to **transform** (합화), the combined element must be in **season** at the month branch and the participating branches must not be **separated** by 충 or other disrupting forces. Otherwise, the combination remains a **half-binding** (합而不化).

## Branch Three Harmonies (삼합, 三合)

Three branches from the same cardinal direction form a complete three-harmony. Two of three = 반합 (half-harmony).

| Group | Element | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| 申子辰 (신자진) | Water (수) | North |
| 亥卯未 (해묘미) | Wood (목) | East |
| 寅午戌 (인오술) | Fire (화) | South |
| 巳酉丑 (사유축) | Metal (금) | West |

> When all three appear in the chart (e.g., 申 + 子 + 辰), the element is **highly empowered**. When two appear, it is a **partial empowerment** (반합), often read as the element "tending toward" that group.

## Directional Harmonies (방합, 方合)

The three branches of a cardinal direction sitting together (without being adjacent) form a 방합. Functionally similar to 삼합 but less concentrated. Sometimes counted alongside 삼합.

## Branch Clashes (충, 沖) — Six Pairs

Direct opposites in the 12-cycle. Brings disruption, change, sudden events, conflict. The two clashing branches **weaken each other**.

| Pair | Element Interaction |
|---|---|
| 子午 (자오) | Water ↔ Fire (water-fire opposition) |
| 丑未 (축미) | Earth ↔ Earth (same element, opposition) |
| 寅申 (인신) | Wood ↔ Metal (wood-metal control) |
| 卯酉 (묘유) | Wood ↔ Metal (wood-metal control) |
| 辰戌 (진술) | Earth ↔ Earth (same element, opposition) |
| 巳亥 (사해) | Fire ↔ Water (fire-water opposition) |

> When 충 appears in natal chart, it often indicates **instability in the areas governed** by those branches. When it appears between natal and a major / annual luck pillar, it often signals an "activating" event for that domain.

## Branch Self-Punishment (자형, 自刑)

Four branches that "punish themselves" when appearing in pairs:

- 辰辰 (진진) — Dragon/Dargon
- 午午 (오오) — Horse/Horse
- 酉酉 (유유) — Rooster/Rooster
- 亥亥 (해해) — Pig/Pig

Indicates inner torment, self-destructive tendencies, difficulty letting go of patterns.

## Branch Six Harms (해, 害) and Six Breaks (파, 破)

Both are subtler than 충. Common in classical texts but less decisive in modern Korean 명리.

| 해 (Harm) | 파 (Break) |
|---|---|
| 子未 (자미) | 子酉 (자유) |
| 丑午 (축오) | 午卯 (오묘) |
| 寅巳 (인사) | 辰丑 (진축) |
| 卯辰 (묘진) | 未戌 (미술) |
| 申亥 (신해) | 申巳 (신사) |
| 酉戌 (유술) | 寅亥 (인해) |

> These relationships are read as "covert friction" (해) and "erosion of trust" (파). Less weight than 충 in most modern Korean readings; classical texts give them more prominence.

## Special Branches (특지지)

- **Four Pure Branches (사정, 四淨):** 子, 卯, 午, 酉 — pure single-element branches. "Pure" because the 본기 alone defines them.
- **Four Stored Branches (사고, 四庫):** 辰, 戌, 丑, 未 — "storehouses" of Earth, the "graveyard" branches. Hold residual qi of seasons.
- **Four Birth Branches (사생, 四生):** 寅, 巳, 申, 亥 — branches where the 12운성's "장생" begins for Yang stems (see `06-twelve-stages.md`).
