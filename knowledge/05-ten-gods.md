# 05 · Ten Gods (십신, 十神)

The Ten Gods are the **lens** through which every other stem and branch in the chart is interpreted in relation to the Day Master. They are derived from the **Five Elements** and **Yin-Yang** relationships.

The Ten Gods are categorized into **five classes**, each with a **yang and yin variant** (totaling 10):

| Class | Yang variant | Yin variant | What it represents |
|---|---|---|---|
| 비겁 (Companion) | 비견 (比肩) | 겁재 (劫財) | Self, peers, competition, siblings |
| 식상 (Output) | 식신 (食神) | 상관 (傷官) | Expression, creativity, output, speech, children (one aspect) |
| 재성 (Wealth) | 편재 (偏財) | 정재 (正財) | Money, possessions, spouse (one aspect), livelihood |
| 관성 (Authority) | 편관 (偏官) | 정관 (正官) | Career, status, discipline, father (one aspect), husband (one aspect) |
| 인성 (Resource) | 편인 (偏印) | 정인 (正印) | Support, learning, mother (one aspect), real estate, authority from above |

## The Five Class Logic

To find which class a given element belongs to as a ten-god of the Day Master, you compare the element of the **other stem** to the **element of the Day Master**:

| Other stem's element | Day Master relation | Class |
|---|---|---|
| Same as Day Master | Self / peer | 비겁 (Companion) |
| Day Master generates it | Output / child | 식상 (Output) |
| Day Master controls it | Wealth | 재성 (Wealth) |
| It controls Day Master | Authority / Office | 관성 (Authority) |
| It generates Day Master | Resource / Support | 인성 (Resource) |

Then, within each class, the **yin-yang polarity** decides the variant:

| Day Master polarity | Other stem polarity | Variant |
|---|---|---|
| Same polarity | Same polarity | 양 (yang) variant — 비견, 식신, 편재, 편관, 편인 |
| Different polarity | Different polarity | 음 (yin) variant — 겁재, 상관, 정재, 정관, 정인 |

> **Polarity rule:** Same polarity → the "편 (indirect)" / "양 (yang)" version. Different polarity → the "정 (direct)" / "음 (yin)" version.

## The Five Classes in Detail

### 1. 비겁 (Companion) — 비견 + 겁재

**Element:** Same as Day Master.

- **비견 (比肩, Pillar)** — Same yang/yang or yin/yin polarity. A peer, a sibling, a co-equal. Implies support, solidarity, but also competition. "I am as I am, and I meet you as a peer."
- **겁재 (劫財, Robber)** — Different polarity within the same element. A rival, a competitor, a "robber of wealth." Often brings competition in love or money; can be a co-worker who takes credit.

**Manifestation:** Sibling relationships, peers, self-reliance, competition, partnership risk. 비겁 is the only "self" class — the more 비겁 in the chart, the more the Day Master asserts its own nature.

**Best when:** Day Master is weak — 비겁 strengthens it.
**Worst when:** Day Master is already strong — 비겁 adds burden and may bring financial loss (via 겁재 "robbery").

### 2. 식상 (Output) — 식신 + 상관

**Element:** Generated by Day Master. (Wood → Fire; Fire → Earth; Earth → Metal; Metal → Water; Water → Wood.)

- **식신 (食神, Eating God)** — Same polarity (yang-yang or yin-yin). Gentle output. Talent, pleasure, slow accumulation, children, food, peaceful expression.
- **상관 (傷官, Hurting Officer)** — Different polarity. Sharp, biting output. Creativity, rebellion, sharp speech, wounds through expression, difficult to control.

**Manifestation:** Creativity, speech, writing, children (one school), eating, what the querent "produces" in the world.

**Best when:** Day Master is strong — 식상 channels the surplus.
**Worst when:** 식상 is too many or too strong, it drains the Day Master and is often read as causing problems with 관성 (e.g., 상관견관 = 상관 directly clashing with 정관, the classical "rebellion against authority" pattern).

### 3. 재성 (Wealth) — 편재 + 정재

**Element:** Controlled by Day Master. (Wood → Earth; Fire → Metal; Earth → Water; Metal → Wood; Water → Fire.)

- **편재 (偏財, Indirect Wealth)** — Same polarity. Windfall, large but variable income, speculative gain, father's wealth, second marriage, the "big catch" partner.
- **정재 (正財, Direct Wealth)** — Different polarity. Salary, stable income, savings, one's own earned money, first marriage (in traditional readings), everyday wealth.

**Manifestation:** Money, possessions, livelihood, the spouse-as-resource (one school), what one can "use" or "have" in the world.

**Best when:** Day Master is strong enough to "hold" the wealth.
**Worst when:** Day Master is weak — wealth comes in but cannot be held, and may bring illness (재다신약).

### 4. 관성 (Authority) — 편관 + 정관

**Element:** Controls the Day Master, per the 상극 (overcoming) cycle in `knowledge/03-five-elements.md` (Wood → Earth → Water → Fire → Metal → Wood, each element controlling the next). Whatever element controls the Day Master's own element is the 관성.

- **편관 (偏官, Seven Killings / 칠살)** — Same polarity. Pressure, competition, discipline through force, military/political authority, the husband (in wife's chart, in one reading), sudden dangers.
- **정관 (正官, Direct Officer)** — Different polarity. Conventional career, government, exam success, gentle authority, husband (in wife's chart, in another reading), father (in son's chart, in another reading), social status.

**Manifestation:** Career, status, discipline, the social order, the father, the husband (depending on school).

**Best when:** Day Master is strong and 관성 is moderate — brings structure and accomplishment.
**Worst when:** 관성 is too strong (especially 칠살) — the querent is oppressed, restricted, persecuted. "Official kills" pattern: 칠살 + 정관 clashing → trouble with authority.

### 5. 인성 (Resource) — 편인 + 정인

**Element:** Generates the Day Master. (Water → Wood; Wood → Fire; Fire → Earth; Earth → Metal; Metal → Water.)

- **편인 (偏印, Indirect Resource / 도인, Owl)** — Same polarity. Unconventional learning, solitary scholarship, intuition, sometimes loneliness, sometimes "the owl star" of strange spiritual gifts.
- **정인 (正印, Direct Resource)** — Different polarity. Education, formal degrees, mother's care, real estate, vehicles, official seals, blessings from elders.

**Manifestation:** Support, learning, mother, real estate, vehicles, official authorization, what sustains the querent from above.

**Best when:** Day Master is weak — 인성 strengthens it.
**Worst when:** 인성 is too strong — it "drowns" the 식상 (output), making the querent dependent, isolated, or over-educated and under-producing. The classical "인성 과다" (印星過多) pattern.

## Ten-God Conflict Patterns

| Pattern | 명 | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 상관견관 (傷官見官) | 상관 meets 정관 | Conflict with authority, discipline, accusations. Classical "rebellious" pattern. |
| 편인식신 (偏印食神) | 편인 meets 식신 | "Owl eats the Eating God" (도인식신) — the unconventional learning star eats the gentle output, often read as: lost output, interrupted creativity, depressive or anxious tendencies. |
| 겁재奪財 (劫財奪財) | 겁재 meets 재성 | Companion class "robs" the wealth — financial loss, partner loss, business partnership risk. |
| 정편관혼잡 (正偏官混雜) | 정관 + 편관 both present | "Mixed" authority star — multiple sources of pressure, multiple careers, divided discipline. |
| 식상생재 (食傷生財) | 식상 + 재성 | Output feeds wealth — natural path: produce → earn. Highly favorable for entrepreneurial types. |
| 인성생신 (印星生身) | 인성 + (Day Master) | Resource strengthens self — the supporting environment. Favorable for students, scholars, those with strong family support. |
