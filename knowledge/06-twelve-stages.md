# 06 · Twelve Life Stages (12운성, 十二運星)

The Twelve Life Stages describe the **cyclical state of an element** as it progresses through the twelve branches. Each stem has its own cycle, starting from a different "장생 (long life / birth)" branch and proceeding in a fixed direction.

The cycle is the same twelve stages; what varies by stem is **where it starts** and which direction it goes.

## The Twelve Stages

| Stage | Korean | Hanja | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 장생 | 長生 | Birth, beginning, fresh energy |
| 2 | 목욕 | 沐浴 | Bathing, vulnerability, exposure, sensuality |
| 3 | 관대 | 冠帶 | Coming-of-age, gaining form, ambition |
| 4 | 건록 | 健祿 | Strong, healthy, earning, in one's prime |
| 5 | 제왕 | 帝旺 | Peak, imperial glory, maximum power |
| 6 | 쇠 | 衰 | Decline, weakening |
| 7 | 병 | 病 | Illness, depletion, rest needed |
| 8 | 사 | 死 | Death, end of a phase |
| 9 | 묘 | 墓 | Tomb, storage, hidden state |
| 10 | 절 | 絕 | Severance, cutoff, lowest point |
| 11 | 태 | 胎 | Embryo, gestation, potential |
| 12 | 양 | 養 | Nurturing, incubation, preparing for birth |

> Note: 묘 (墓, tomb) is sometimes written as 고 (庫, storehouse) in some schools, but the meanings are similar. *(Corrected 2026-09-20 — external report review, 4th pass: 양 (養, nurturing) was previously included in this equivalence too, but 養 is never written 庫 — only 墓/묘 corresponds to the four storage branches.)*

## Direction of the Cycle

- **Yang stems** (甲, 丙, 戊, 庚, 壬) cycle **forward** through the 12 branches from their 장생 start.
- **Yin stems** (乙, 丁, 己, 辛, 癸) cycle **backward** through the 12 branches from their 장생 start.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 甲 갑 | Wood | Yang | 亥 (해, Pig) | Forward |
| 乙 을 | Wood | Yin | 午 (오, Horse) | Backward |
| 丙 병 | Fire | Yang | 寅 (인, Tiger) | Forward |
| 丁 정 | Fire | Yin | 酉 (유, Rooster) | Backward |
| 戊 무 | Earth | Yang | 寅 (인, Tiger) | Forward |
| 己 기 | Earth | Yin | 酉 (유, Rooster) | Backward |
| 庚 경 | Metal | Yang | 巳 (사, Snake) | Forward |
| 辛 신 | Metal | Yin | 子 (자, Rat) | Backward |
| 壬 임 | Water | Yang | 申 (신, Monkey) | Forward |

> The starting point determines **which branch is the Day Master's "birth" / "self-realization"** branch. For example, a 甲 day master is "born" in 亥 and reaches peak (제왕) at 卯.

## Reading the Twelve Stages

The stages are interpreted as the **state of the Day Master (or any element being tracked)** in that branch:

- **장생 (Birth):** The energy is fresh, beginning, supported by the season or environment. A good sign.
- **목욕 (Bathing):** The energy is exposed, vulnerable, sensuous, sometimes unstable. A mixed sign — pleasure but also risk.
- **관대 (Coming-of-Age):** The energy is taking form, ambitious, ready. A positive sign.
- **건록 (Strong / Earning):** The energy is at full strength, accumulating, in one's prime. Very positive for career, wealth.
- **제왕 (Peak):** The energy is at maximum. **Powerful but also at the turning point** — peak precedes decline. Excessive in one direction.
- **쇠 (Decline):** The energy is past peak, weakening. Rest is needed.
- **병 (Illness):** The energy is depleted, requires recovery. Often a difficult period.
- **사 (Death):** The phase is ending. New cycle ahead, but in this period things are at their lowest.
- **묘 (Tomb):** The energy is stored, hidden, not active. Often a period of waiting, accumulation.
- **절 (Cutoff):** The energy is severed, the lowest point. Often a period of nothing — or a clean break.
- **태 (Embryo):** New energy is being formed, potential is being developed. Often a quiet period of preparation.
- **양 (Nurturing):** The energy is being cultivated, ready to be born into a new 장생 phase.

## Day Master Strength by Stage (Cheat Sheet)

For the **Day Master itself**, the stages that strengthen the chart:

- **Strongest stages (Day Master is supported):** 장생, 목욕, 관대, 건록, 제왕.
- **Weakest stages (Day Master is unsupported):** 쇠, 병, 사, 묘, 절.
- **Transitional stages (mixed):** 태, 양.

> A Day Master in **건록 or 제왕** in the month branch is at **seasonal peak** and is considered **신강 (身強, strong Day Master)**. A Day Master in **사, 묘, 절, 병, or 쇠** in the month branch is **seasonally weak** and considered **신약 (身弱, weak Day Master)** unless supported by other factors.

> **Strength scoring uses the season/element relation, not this stage (decided 2026-09-26, audit finding N-5).** For **yang** stems the stage above tracks the season closely. For **yin** stems it runs the backward 음생양사 (陰生陽死) cycle, so it can point the opposite way from the season: 乙 is at 장생 in 午 (a Fire month that drains Wood) and at 사 in 亥 (a Water month that feeds Wood). The 적천수 commentary tradition (임철초) is critical of using 음장생 for strength; that citation comes from the audit and has not been checked against a primary text in this repo. The governing criterion for 신강/신약 is therefore `09-interpretation-method.md` Step 2: is the month branch the Day Master's own element or peak season, its resource, or an off-season (output / wealth / authority) element? The stage in this file stays the **descriptive** 12운성 reading (temperament, 대운/세운 stage narratives). It is no longer the seasonal input to the strength verdict. The engine follows this rule: `strength.month_relation()`.

## 12 Stages + Ten Gods

The 12 stages are sometimes layered on top of the 십신 (Ten Gods) reading:

- A **관성 (Officer)** in **건록** is a "strong, well-established" career force.
- A **재성 (Wealth)** in **제왕** is wealth at peak — but a turning point.
- A **인성 (Resource)** in **사** is a depleted resource — the support has run out.
- A **식상 (Output)** in **목욕** is exposed, sensual, but unstable.

- To find the Day Master's stage in any branch → look up the appropriate Stem table.
