# 12 · Career & Vocation (직업론, 職業論)

Career in Saju is not read from a single "job star." It is a synthesis of three
layers:

1. **The Day Master (일간, 日干) element and what the chart *needs*.** The
   element mix of the chart, and especially the favorable element (용신, 用神),
   points to the *kind of work environment* that keeps the person in balance —
   not just the field that matches the Day Master's own element. *(see
   `knowledge/09-interpretation-method.md` Step 3)*
2. **The ten-god (십신, 十神) balance.** Which of the five classes — 비겁 (比劫),
   식상 (食傷), 재성 (財星), 관성 (官星), 인성 (印星) — is most developed shapes the
   *mode* of work (creating vs. managing vs. owning vs. advising vs. competing).
   *(see `knowledge/05-ten-gods.md`)*
3. **The timing layer (대운, 大運).** The major-luck decades open and close
   windows for a field or a working style; a natal aptitude may only become
   usable when the 대운 supports it. *(see `knowledge/08-luck-pillars.md`)*

Follow `knowledge/09-interpretation-method.md` Steps 3–4 before using this file:
strength first, then 용신, then the ten-god decode.

> All statements here are **tendencies and influences**, not job predictions or
> guarantees of success. See `## Scope & Limits`.

## Element → Industry Families

Each element's industry family is derived from the **nature of the element** as
set out in `knowledge/03-five-elements.md` (its season, direction, and its role
in the generating/overcoming cycles). The specific mapping of an element to a
list of modern occupations is **modern 명리 (命理) convention** — the classical
texts describe the element's character, and Korean practitioners extend that
character to contemporary industries. Treat the field lists as illustrative of
the element's *flavour of work*, not a closed catalogue.

### Wood

**Nature:** growth, branching, cultivation, upward structure. Wood is the Spring
element that pushes upward and outward, nourished into being and then feeding
Fire *(see `knowledge/03-five-elements.md`)*. Work with a Wood character grows
something over time, develops people or ideas, and builds structure that keeps
extending.

- education & training
- healthcare & wellness
- design, writing & publishing
- social & environmental work
- HR / people development
- horticulture, forestry, textiles

### Fire

**Nature:** visibility, expression, transformation, energy. Fire is the Summer
element of maximum outward radiance; it makes things seen, converts fuel into
light and heat, and cannot act in private *(see
`knowledge/03-five-elements.md`)*. Work with a Fire character is public-facing,
expressive, and energising to an audience.

- leadership & public-facing roles
- media, performing & broadcast arts
- marketing, PR & advertising
- teaching, coaching & public speaking
- entrepreneurship & product evangelism
- energy, lighting, hospitality front-of-house
- beauty & aesthetics

### Earth

**Nature:** holding, storing, mediating, grounding. Earth sits at the centre,
receives Fire's ash and yields Metal; it is the element that contains,
stabilises, and stands between *(see `knowledge/03-five-elements.md`)*. Work with
an Earth character holds assets, mediates between parties, and provides a stable
base others rely on.

- real estate & property
- construction & civil works
- hospitality & food
- finance, accounting & insurance
- counselling, mediation & pastoral care
- operations, logistics & project management
- agriculture & land

### Metal

**Nature:** precision, refinement, boundary, judgement. Metal is the Autumn
element that cuts, separates, and finishes; it is refined out of Earth and, in
the overcoming cycle, is the element that prunes Wood *(see
`knowledge/03-five-elements.md`)*. Work with a Metal character demands accuracy,
enforces a standard, and draws clean lines.

- law & governance
- engineering & technology infrastructure
- medicine, surgery & diagnostics
- finance, investment & trading
- quality, audit & forensic analysis
- security, defence & policing
- metalwork, jewellery, instruments

### Water

**Nature:** flow, connection, depth, movement. Water is the Winter element that
moves downward and around obstacles, connects separated things, and runs deep;
Metal condenses into it and it nourishes Wood *(see
`knowledge/03-five-elements.md`)*. Work with a Water character moves information,
goods, or people, connects networks, and works below the visible surface.

- strategy, consulting & research
- diplomacy & international trade
- psychology, counselling & therapy
- arts, curation & creative research
- journalism & investigation
- logistics, shipping, travel & mobility
- data, networks & platforms

## Ten-God → Career Mode

The dominant ten-god class describes *how* the person works, independent of the
field. Derived from the class manifestations in `knowledge/05-ten-gods.md`.

| Dominant class | Career mode |
|---|---|
| 재성 (Wealth) | Business, sales, owning and growing resources — turning effort into held assets and livelihood. |
| 관성 (Authority) | Institutions, licensed authority, management, government — operating inside a structure of accountable rank. |
| 식상 (Output) | Creating, expressing, teaching, making, performing — producing something that goes out into the world. |
| 인성 (Resource) | Research, advisory, knowledge / credentialed practice, curation — working from accumulated learning and authorisation. |
| 비겁 (Companion) | Peer / team / competitive settings, independent operator, partnerships — working alongside or against equals on one's own terms. |

*(see `knowledge/05-ten-gods.md` for each class's full manifestation and its
best/worst conditions relative to Day Master strength)*

## 용신 vs. Day Master for Career Choice

The classical priority — from the 용신 logic of the 궁통보감 (窮通寶鑑) lineage as
applied in `knowledge/09-interpretation-method.md` Step 3 — is to **align work
with what the chart *needs* (용신 / 희신, 喜神), not merely with the Day Master's own
element.** Choosing a field that reinforces an already-dominant element pushes
the chart further out of balance.

- **Too-strong Day Master (신강, 身強):** favour fields that **drain or channel**
  the Day Master — Output (식상), Wealth (재성), or Officer (관성) work. The
  surplus self-energy needs an outlet; work that expresses, earns, or takes on
  accountable structure uses it productively. *(see
  `knowledge/09-interpretation-method.md` Step 3, 신강 branch)*
- **Too-weak Day Master (신약, 身弱):** favour fields aligned with **Resource
  (인성) or Companion (비겁)** first — learning-based roles, credentialed
  practice, supported or team environments — so the self is strengthened before
  it is asked to carry heavy Wealth or Officer loads. *(see
  `knowledge/09-interpretation-method.md` Step 3, 신약 branch)*

In practice: identify the 용신 element from Step 3, map it through the
**Element → Industry Families** list above, and cross-check the result against
the **Ten-God → Career Mode** the same element produces for this Day Master.

## Employment vs. Entrepreneurship

Signals below indicate *tendency and fit*, not capacity or outcome.

- **Strong Day Master + visible 식상 / 재성:** the chart can carry the
  variability of independent work — there is surplus self-energy, a channel for
  output, and a path from output to earnings (식상생재, see
  `knowledge/05-ten-gods.md`). Independent or founder-style work is a plausible
  fit.
- **Weak Day Master, or 관성-led chart:** institutional employment tends to fit
  better *first* — a structured environment supplies the stability and rank
  that the chart does not generate internally. Independence may become viable
  later if a 대운 strengthens the Day Master.
- **겁재 (劫財) prominent:** exercise partnership caution. The 겁재奪財 (劫財奪財)
  pattern — Companion class "robbing" the Wealth — is read as financial and
  partnership risk *(see `knowledge/13-wealth-and-business.md` and
  `knowledge/05-ten-gods.md`)*. Sole operation, or partnerships with very clear
  boundaries, tend to be safer.

## Skill-Levers by Dominant Ten-God

This section is a **modern practical gloss**, not classical doctrine: each lever
is a contemporary development suggestion aligned with the class manifestations in
`knowledge/05-ten-gods.md`, offered as reflection rather than career advice (see
`## Scope & Limits`). The one practical development lever most aligned with each
dominant class:

- **Companion (비겁):** collaboration and honest self-assessment — knowing where
  one stands relative to peers.
- **Output (식상):** craft and a sustainable communication cadence — regular
  production without draining the self.
- **Wealth (재성):** pricing and resource stewardship — valuing the work and
  keeping what it earns.
- **Officer (관성):** systems and accountable leadership — building structure
  and standing behind decisions.
- **Resource (인성):** depth study and synthesis — turning accumulated learning
  into usable output rather than stockpiling it.

## Scope & Limits

- This file describes a **vocational tendency** — the flavour of work and
  working style a chart leans toward. It is **not** a prediction of a specific
  job, employer, or title, and **not** a guarantee (or denial) of career
  success.
- It is **not career advice.** Real career decisions depend on skills,
  circumstances, opportunity, and choice — none of which a chart determines.
- The element → occupation lists are **modern 명리 convention**, not classical
  doctrine. The classical basis is the element's *character* (`03-five-elements.md`)
  and the ten-god *manifestations* (`05-ten-gods.md`); the extension to
  contemporary industries is interpretive.
- Where a chart is genuinely ambiguous, say so ("전통 해석이 확립되지
  않았습니다") rather than forcing a single field.

## How to Use This File

- Read this file **after Step 4** of `knowledge/09-interpretation-method.md` —
  the Day Master, its strength, the 용신, and the ten-god decode must already be
  in hand.
- Combine three reads: (1) the **element family** for the 용신 / 희신, (2) the
  **ten-god career mode** for the dominant class, (3) the **용신 vs. Day Master**
  test to confirm the field balances rather than inflates the chart.
- For entrepreneurship-vs-employment questions, add the
  **Employment vs. Entrepreneurship** signals and cross-ref
  `knowledge/13-wealth-and-business.md`.
- Hand all **timing** questions ("when should I switch?", "is this decade good
  for launching?") to `knowledge/08-luck-pillars.md` — this file is natal
  aptitude only.
