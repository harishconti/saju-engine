# 13 · Wealth & Business (재물론, 財物論)

Wealth in Saju is read from the **재성 (財星, Wealth star)** — the class of stems
and branches the Day Master (일간, 日干) controls — but never in isolation. How
money is *earned*, whether it can be *held*, and *when* it flows are three
separate questions, answered by three different reads:

1. **The style of income** — 정재 (正財) vs. 편재 (偏財) within the 재성 class.
2. **The producing chain** — whether 식상 (食傷, Output) feeds the 재성, whether
   the 재성 in turn feeds 관성 (官星, Authority), and whether wealth sits in a
   storehouse (재고, 財庫).
3. **The carrying capacity** — whether the Day Master is strong enough, with
   비겁 (比劫, Companion) and 인성 (印星, Resource) support, to hold what the
   재성 brings in.

Follow `knowledge/09-interpretation-method.md` Steps 3–5 before using this file:
Day Master strength first, then the 용신 (用神, favorable element) / 희신 (喜神,
supporting element), then the ten-god decode.

> Every statement here is a **tendency or influence**, not a prediction of an
> amount, a gain, or a loss. See `## Scope & Limits`.

## 정재 vs. 편재 — Two Income Styles

Both are the 재성 (財星) class — the element the Day Master controls — split by
yin-yang polarity *(see `knowledge/05-ten-gods.md`, 재성 section)*.

| | 정재 (正財, Direct Wealth) | 편재 (偏財, Indirect Wealth) |
|---|---|---|
| Polarity to Day Master | Different polarity | Same polarity |
| Income shape | Salary, steady cash flow, savings, disciplined revenue | Deal-making, commissions, trading, side income, windfalls |
| Variance | Low — predictable, incremental | High — lumpy, "the big catch" |
| Feel | One's own earned, accounted money | Money that moves through many hands; speculative gain |
| Typical vehicle | Employment, a fixed book of business, rent from a held asset | Sales, brokerage, ventures, markets, multiple concurrent streams |

- A chart weighted to **정재** tends to build wealth by accumulation and
  retention; risk tolerance is lower and concentration in one reliable source
  is natural.
- A chart weighted to **편재** tends to build wealth by turnover and
  opportunity capture; several streams at once suit it better than one, and
  it is more comfortable with variance.
- **Both present (재성 혼잡, 混雜):** income comes from mixed sources — a base plus
  variable upside — which can be stabilising or scattering depending on Day
  Master strength (below).

Neither style is "better"; each names a *mode*, and the favourable style is the
one the rest of the chart can support.

## Wealth-Producing Chains

These are the classical ten-god chains that describe **how** 재성 is generated
and **where** it goes. The first two follow directly from the generating cycle
(상생, 相生) in `knowledge/03-five-elements.md` — Output generates Wealth, Wealth
generates Authority — with the ten-god naming taken from
`knowledge/05-ten-gods.md`; the third ties to the storehouse logic in
`knowledge/02-branches.md`.

### 식상생재 (食傷生財) — Output feeds Wealth

식상 (食傷, Output — 식신 (食神) + 상관 (傷官)) is the element the Day Master
*generates*; the
재성 is the element the Day Master *controls*, and in the generating cycle
Output generates Wealth *(see `knowledge/03-five-elements.md`)*. So skill,
production, or expression converts into earnings: **produce → earn**.
`knowledge/05-ten-gods.md` reads this chain as "highly favorable for
entrepreneurial types" — there is a built-in path from what the person makes to
what they are paid. It works best when the Day Master is strong enough to
sustain the output (see next section).

### 재생관 (財生官) — Wealth funds status

In the generating cycle the 재성 generates the 관성 (官星, Authority — the
element that controls the Day Master) *(see `knowledge/03-five-elements.md`)*.
Money is converted into position, institutional standing, licensure, or office.
A chart with a clear 식상 → 재성 → 관성 run has a full pipeline: produce, earn,
and turn earnings into standing. When the Day Master is weak, 재생관 can become
a drain — wealth strengthens the very star that pressures the self.

### 재고 (財庫) — Wealth in the storehouse

When the 재성 element sits as the qi of one of the four stored branches
(사고, 四庫 — 辰, 戌, 丑, 未) *(see `knowledge/02-branches.md`, Special
Branches)*, wealth is read as **stored rather than circulating**: delayed,
accumulated, latent. The traditional reading is that the storehouse "opens" —
releases its wealth — when it is triggered by a 충 (沖, clash) or 형 (刑,
punishment) from the natal chart, a 대운 (大運), or a 세운 (歲運) *(see
`knowledge/02-branches.md` for the 충/형 pairs; `knowledge/08-luck-pillars.md`
for luck-period triggers)*. This "storehouse opens on the clash" rule is
**classical doctrine** (연해자평 / 명리 school); the modern caution is that a
clash to a storehouse can also *scatter* the stored qi rather than release it
cleanly, and the classics do not settle which outcome applies to a given chart
— where a chart is ambiguous, say "전통 해석이 확립되지 않았습니다".

## Can the Day Master Hold Wealth?

재성 in the chart is only usable if the Day Master can carry it *(see
`knowledge/09-interpretation-method.md` Steps 2–3)*.

- **Strong Day Master (신강, 身強):** carries 재성 (財星) and 관성 (官星)
  directly. Wealth that comes in can be held, and Authority that comes with it
  can be borne. Such a chart benefits from *more* 재성/관성 activity, because it
  channels surplus self-energy *(see `knowledge/05-ten-gods.md` — 재성 "best
  when Day Master is strong enough to hold the wealth")*.
- **Weak Day Master (신약, 身弱):** needs 인성 (印星, Resource) or 비겁
  (比劫, Companion) support **first** — learning, credentials, family backing,
  peers, a strengthening 대운 — before it is asked to carry a heavy 재성 load.
- **재다신약 (財多身弱) — "much wealth, weak self":** when 재성 is abundant but
  the Day Master is weak, the classical reading is that **money becomes a
  burden**. Wealth flows in but cannot be retained; the effort of chasing and
  managing it drains the self, and the pattern is traditionally associated with
  stress and health strain *(see `knowledge/05-ten-gods.md` — "wealth comes in
  but cannot be held, and may bring illness (재다신약)")*. The remedy in the
  classics is to strengthen the self (비겁 / 인성), not to pursue more 재성.

The 재성 that helps a chart is 재성 that matches its capacity — abundant 재성 in
a weak chart is a 기신 (忌神, unfavorable influence) pattern, not a blessing.

## 겁재奪財 — Partnership & Shared-Money Risk

겁재 (劫財, Robber) is the yin-polarity variant of the 비겁 (比劫, Companion)
class — a rival within the same element as the Day Master. The
**겁재奪財 (劫財奪財)** pattern from `knowledge/05-ten-gods.md`'s conflict table
— "Companion class 'robs' the wealth" — reads a 비겁-heavy chart, or a 대운/세운
that adds 비겁, as **financial loss, partner loss, and partnership breakdown**,
especially where money is *shared*: business partners, family businesses,
pooled investments, informal lending among peers.

The mechanism: the 비겁 competes with the Day Master for the same 재성, so
another equal has a claim on the same pool of money.

**Mitigations** (modern practice, consistent with the classical reading that
the risk is to *shared and undefined* wealth):

- Put **roles and ownership percentages in writing** before money moves.
- Keep **separate accounts**; do not commingle personal and venture funds.
- Earn through a **structured product or service** with clear pricing, not
  through informal or handshake arrangements.
- Prefer **sole operation**, or partnerships with hard boundaries, over
  loosely-shared ventures — this is the same signal flagged in
  `knowledge/12-career-and-vocation.md` under Employment vs. Entrepreneurship.
- Treat 겁재 / 기신 (忌神) 대운 as periods to **defend rather than expand**
  shared commitments (see Wealth Preservation).

## Wealth Timing

Read wealth-active periods from the luck layers *(see
`knowledge/08-luck-pillars.md`)*:

- A **대운 (大運)** or **세운 (歲運)** whose stem or branch is 재성 (財星) to the
  Day Master tends to raise wealth activity — earning, spending, and money
  decisions all become more prominent. Whether that activity is *net
  favourable* depends on Day Master strength: a strong Day Master uses a 재성
  period productively; a weak one experiences it as pressure (재다신약, above).
- A period that runs the chart's **용신 (用神) / 희신 (喜神)** element supports
  wealth outcomes even when it is not itself a 재성 period, because the chart is
  in better balance.
- **재고 (財庫)** storehouse branches: watch for a 대운/세운 branch that
  **clashes or punishes** a natal storehouse holding the 재성 element — the
  traditional "storehouse opens" timing (with the scatter caveat from Wealth-
  Producing Chains).
- **House purchase and other large, long-term commitments** key to
  **인성 (印星)** — the Resource star governs property, deeds, seals, and
  contracts *(see `knowledge/05-ten-gods.md`, 인성 section)*. The traditional
  preference is to place such commitments where 인성 is active **and** the year
  runs the favourable element, rather than in a bare 재성 year.

This file gives the *reading method*; the actual period-by-period work belongs
in `knowledge/08-luck-pillars.md`.

## Wealth Preservation

Framed as tendencies in *stewardship style*, not investment instructions (see
`## Scope & Limits`).

- **Strong Day Master:** the chart can carry concentration, but the classical
  caution is not to hold *beyond what the chart supports* — diversify enough
  that no single loss is structural. Favour custodianship aligned with the
  **용신 (用神) / 희신 (喜神)** element (its direction, its industry family via
  `knowledge/12-career-and-vocation.md`).
- **Weak Day Master (재다신약 pattern):** prioritise **retention over
  acquisition** — fewer, simpler holdings; strengthen the self (인성 / 비겁
  activities — study, credentials, trusted peers) before taking on more.
- **겁재 (劫財) or 기신 (忌神) decades:** **defend rather than expand.** Avoid
  new shared ventures, formalise or wind down loose partnerships, keep
  liquidity, and postpone large speculative commitments to a more favourable
  period.
- **Storehouse (재고) charts:** wealth accumulates quietly and releases in
  bursts; the preservation task is to be *ready* for the release windows rather
  than expecting steady flow.

## Scope & Limits

- This file describes **tendencies in how money is earned, held, and timed** —
  the income style a chart leans toward, whether it can carry a wealth load,
  and which periods raise wealth activity.
- It does **not** predict amounts, gains, losses, or business outcomes, and it
  is **not financial, investment, or business advice.** Per `CLAUDE.md` Ground
  Rule 5, Saju can suggest *tendencies* in livelihood; it does not diagnose
  finances or guarantee any financial result. Real outcomes depend on skills,
  capital, markets, timing, and choice — none of which a chart determines.
- The element → industry extensions referenced here are **modern 명리 (命理)
  convention**; the classical basis is the 재성 (財星) manifestations
  (`05-ten-gods.md`) and the five-element cycles (`03-five-elements.md`).
- The "storehouse opens on the clash" rule is classical, but the classics do
  **not** settle release-vs-scatter for a given chart — flag genuine ambiguity
  with "전통 해석이 확립되지 않았습니다" rather than forcing a call.
- All readings are **potentials and influences**, not predictions of
  inevitable events (`CLAUDE.md` Ground Rule 4).

## How to Use This File

- Read it **after Step 5** of `knowledge/09-interpretation-method.md` — Day
  Master, strength, 용신 / 희신, and the ten-god decode must already be in hand.
- Combine three reads: (1) the **income style** (정재 vs. 편재) for the 재성
  present, (2) the **producing chains** (식상생재 / 재생관 / 재고) that are
  actually formed in the chart, (3) the **carrying-capacity** test (신강/신약,
  재다신약) to judge whether the 재성 helps or burdens.
- Pair with `knowledge/12-career-and-vocation.md` for the **earning vehicle**
  (the field and working mode) and with `knowledge/08-luck-pillars.md` for the
  **when** (period-by-period wealth timing).
- For partnership and shared-money questions, use **겁재奪財** together with the
  Employment vs. Entrepreneurship signals in
  `knowledge/12-career-and-vocation.md`.
