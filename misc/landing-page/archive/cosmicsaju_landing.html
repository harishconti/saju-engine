What is Saju?

Ancient Wisdom.
AI Precision.

Saju (四柱, Four Pillars of Destiny) is an East Asian birth chart system that maps your destiny using the exact year, month, day and hour of your birth. Unlike Western horoscopes, Saju is highly specific — no two people share the same chart.

CosmicSaju combines traditional Saju interpretation with AI analysis to deliver structured, actionable reports — not vague predictions.

FAQ

Common Questions

What birth details do I need? +

Date of birth (day/month/year), exact birth time (hour and minute if possible), and city/country of birth. Birth time is important for Saju accuracy — check your birth certificate if available. An approximate time still gives a strong reading.

What if I don't know my exact birth time? +

Enter your best estimate and mention this in the "special focus" field. A 2–3 hour window usually still gives accurate career, wealth, and personality readings. The hour pillar affects the final section of life most directly.

Which report should I choose? +

If you want an overview of your elemental strengths, career arc, and decade cycles — choose Essential (₹799). If you want year-by-year timing, relationship analysis, business launch windows, and an audio walkthrough — choose Deep Destiny (₹1499).

What languages are available? +

Reports are available in English and Tamil. Mention your preference in the form. The Deep Destiny audio summary is currently in English only — Tamil audio coming soon.

How is this different from free astrology apps? +

Free apps give you a generic chart dump with no interpretation. CosmicSaju provides a structured, AI-analyzed report written specifically for your chart — with plain-language explanations of what each element means for your actual life, career, and relationships.

What is your refund policy? +

Full refund available within 2 hours of payment if report generation has not yet begun. Once the report is delivered, refunds are not issued — but we will redo any section if there is a factual data error. Our goal is your complete satisfaction.
