What the Terms Mean

  • Unfavourable Element (Unfavorable Element): the element that unbalances your chart — minimise its influence
  • Day Master: you — the reference point the whole chart is read against
  • Favourable Element (Favorable Element): the element your chart most needs for balance — lean toward it. It is the working target for choices about work, place, colour, and timing.
  • Primary hidden stem (main qi): the main hidden stem of a branch — its dominant inner force
  • Companion: a peer or equal — support, solidarity, and rivalry in equal measure
  • Direct Wealth: steady, earned income and everyday resources — salary and savings
  • Direct Resource: support and knowledge — education, mentors, care, property, credentials
  • Hurting Officer: sharp output — bold expression, creativity, friction with rules and authority
  • Annual Luck: the theme of a single year
  • Major Luck: roughly ten-year life chapters that colour a whole span
  • Peach Blossom: charm and magnetism — attractiveness, and sometimes distraction or scandal
  • Travelling Horse (Post Horse): movement — travel, relocation, changing scenes, a mobile life
  • Canopy: solitude and depth — art, scholarship, spirituality, time alone
  • Robber (Rob Wealth): a rival for the same resources — competition in money or love
