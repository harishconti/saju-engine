Carrying capacity: Strong Day Master — you can carry Wealth (재성) and Authority (관성) directly; wealth that comes in can be held. The caution is not to hold beyond what the chart supports — diversify so no single loss is structural.

Partnership & shared-money risk: Your chart carries 6 Companion stems — the peer-drains-wealth (겁재奪財) pattern reads shared and loosely-defined money as contested. Put ownership percentages in writing, keep separate accounts, and prefer sole operation or hard-boundary partnerships.

Large, long-term commitments (property, deeds, contracts) key to Resource in the classics — prefer placing them where Resource (인성) is active and the year runs the favorable element, not in a bare Wealth (재성) year. Storehouse (재고) charts accumulate quietly and release in bursts: be ready for the release windows rather than expecting steady flow.
